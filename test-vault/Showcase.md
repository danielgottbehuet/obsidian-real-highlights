Reading is easier when the ==important ideas stand out== at a glance. A physical highlighter never leaves a perfect rectangle: ==the edges are slightly irregular==, the ends are slanted, and ==where the pen touches down, a little more ink collects==.

==Long passages that wrap across several lines get their own stroke on every visible line, just like marking a printed page with a real pen.==

Colors work too: ==🔴urgent==, ==🟠follow up==, ==🟡remember==, ==🟢done==, ==🔵reference== and ==🟣idea==.
